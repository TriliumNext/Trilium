title: Notifications
---

A bell in the tab bar collects notifications from Trilium and from scripts. Its badge counts the ones you have not seen yet, a notification opens the note it is about, and _Mark all as read_ and _Clear all_ keep the list short. Notifications also arrive as a toast with a sound, which _Silent_ turns off on one device. On a phone, the bell is in the launch bar.

Notifications sync between your devices. A notification about a protected note shows its private text only while the protected session is open.

A notification of high importance can be forwarded to a webhook, such as a push service for your phone, or to a script of your own, after a grace time in which you can read it in Trilium instead. The channels are set up on the new _Notifications_ page of the settings, with the sound and the categories of notifications you want.

Scripts raise and update notifications through `api.notifications`, with buttons that run actions of their own.
