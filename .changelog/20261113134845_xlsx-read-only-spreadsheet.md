type: improvement
area: Spreadsheet

An Excel workbook stored as a File note or opened as an attachment now opens in a read-only spreadsheet across the full width, with its sheets in tabs and a grid that stays fast however large the workbook is, instead of a converted page that froze on large files. In the note list and the list of attachments it is previewed by the top-left corner of its first sheet, reaching the edges of its card, and an office document shown again is taken from the cache instead of being converted again.
