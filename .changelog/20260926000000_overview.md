This is the release where a text note stops being a single document. Notes can be embedded, quoted and referenced inside other notes and edited where they appear, laid out in columns and tabs, and mixed with drawings, attached files and video. Tables in text notes get the tools they had been missing, from merged cells to sorting, and the old layout is gone, a year after the new one replaced it.

Around the editor, the parts of the app that everyone touches were rebuilt: the context menus and the note autocomplete, the full search and the jump dialog, the launch bar and the tree. Shared pages look like the app again and render collections with their own views, and the calendar and geo map collections get the properties dialogs the board already had.

The AI integration becomes something to trust with a real database: every tool call asks before it reads or changes a note, a ChatGPT Codex subscription can stand in for an API key, and local models get web search through a search engine of your choice. On phones, iOS has a new layout and both apps navigate without a sidebar.
