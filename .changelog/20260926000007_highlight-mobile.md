On a phone, the sidebar was both the note tree and the only way around the app, and it covered the note every time it opened. The iOS and Android apps get a [navigation system that does without it](https://github.com/TriliumNext/Trilium/pull/12279), and iOS gets a [layout redesigned](https://github.com/TriliumNext/Trilium/pull/12263) in the style of iOS 26.

While editing, the formatting toolbar floats over the note as a compact island, shown only while you type, and brings the widget, link and list toolbars along.
