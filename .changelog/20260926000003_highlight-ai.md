**AI** — ChatGPT Codex subscriptions (with a free plan) and OpenRouter join the providers. [Custom search engines](https://github.com/TriliumNext/Trilium/pull/11878) (Brave Search, Tavily, Exa, Serper, Perplexity, or a self-hosted SearXNG) give web search to local models and to providers without one, such as DeepSeek. [Permissions for tool calls](https://github.com/TriliumNext/Trilium/pull/12116): before the AI reads or changes your notes, the chat shows what it is about to do and you allow or deny it, for one call or for the rest of the chat; the Tools menu has three modes: always ask, ask only before changes, or never ask. This works with API keys and with subscription providers such as Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot and Antigravity.
