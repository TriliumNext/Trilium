type: highlight
title: A layout made for tablets
---

On a tablet, in either orientation, the [mobile version](https://docs.triliumnotes.org/user-guide/setup/mobile-frontend) now lays the screen out like the desktop version:

*   The note tree is docked beside the note. A button in the launch bar hides it, and its edge can be dragged to resize it.
*   The tabs are in the desktop's tab bar, and the launch bar has the desktop's launchers.
*   The right sidebar is available, in place of the phone's note panel.
*   Dialogs are centered on the screen, with their list on the left.
