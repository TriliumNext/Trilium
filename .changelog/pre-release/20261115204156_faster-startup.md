title: A faster start, on desktop and on phones
---

Trilium now starts noticeably faster. It shows the launch bar, the tabs and the note tree first, and loads the rest only once you use it. This applies to phones too.

The difference is largest on slower devices. Simulating a slower device on a 20 Mbit/s connection, a note is ready to edit in 2.5 s instead of 5.2 s on desktop, and in 2.3 s instead of 4.8 s on a phone. On a fast computer, it is ready about 30% sooner.
