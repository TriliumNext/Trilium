# Trilium's sample

wardley-beta
    title Tea Shop

    anchor Customers [0.95, 0.63]
    anchor Business [0.95, 0.27]

    component Cup of Tea [0.79, 0.61]
    component Tea [0.63, 0.81]

    Customers -> Cup of Tea
    Cup of Tea -> Tea

==>
